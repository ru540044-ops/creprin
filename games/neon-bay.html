    Neon Bay

    Nova and Cal: two outlaws, one sunburnt bay. Hit the QWIK 24, run boats into the Glades, go viral on Buzzr, and shake the chopper before sunrise.

    Move
    W A S D or arrows, Shift to sprint, Space to jump. Walk off the pier to swim
    Drive
    E to take a car, boat or jet ski, Space to brake, R for radio
    Duo
    C switches between Nova and Cal. Your partner rides along and shoots back. X uses the hero's special: Nova slows time and steadies her aim, Cal slows time and grips the road
    Weapons
    Click or F to shoot, drive-bys too. Q or hold Tab for the wheel, 1 to 4
    Crime
    Hold FIRE inside a red QWIK 24 ring to rob it. Break line of sight to lose the cops
    Shops
    Orange rings are Bay Customs: engine, armor, paint, underglow and repairs. Purple rings are THREADS for clothes and hair
    Phone
    P for Buzzr, M for the map, V cycles the camera including first person
    Touch
    Left thumb steers, right side looks, buttons for the rest
